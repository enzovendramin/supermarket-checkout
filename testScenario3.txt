# Focused scenario for two features not covered by scenarios 1 and 2:
#
#   1. Quantity-based pricing (R3): a bulk discount that applies only once the
#      purchased quantity reaches a threshold.
#   2. Smart logistics (R10): booking 2-hour delivery slots with anti-overbooking
#      (capacity = 2 vehicles per slot) and dynamic pricing (peak surcharge,
#      "eco" discount when a truck is already nearby).
#
# Lines starting with '#' are comments and must be ignored by runTest.
# =========================================================================


# ---- 1. Manager bootstraps and configures a bulk discount ----
login ceo 123456789
setup
registerCashier Carl Cash carl carlpwd
registerCustomer Eve Stone eve "9 rue Test" evepwd
addItem rice grocery 2.00 1.00 100
# 25% off the unit price from 10 units onwards (R3)
setQuantityDiscount rice 10 25

# ---- 2. R10: delivery-slot booking with anti-overbooking ----
# Capacity is 2 vehicles per slot; the third booking of the same slot is refused.
bookDeliverySlot 8
bookDeliverySlot 8
bookDeliverySlot 8

# ---- 3. R10: dynamic pricing quotes (base flat fee €15) ----
quoteDeliverySlot 18 false
quoteDeliverySlot 10 true
quoteDeliverySlot 10 false
logout


# ---- 4. R3 in a real checkout: 10 units of rice get the bulk price ----
login carl carlpwd
startCheckout eve
scanItem rice 10
# 10 x (2.00 - 25%) = 10 x 1.50 = 15.00
computeBill
simulatePayment SUCCESS
pay 4242424242424242 1234
logout


# ---- 5. Manager inspects revenue (only the €15.00 rice sale) ----
login ceo 123456789
showRevenue
logout
